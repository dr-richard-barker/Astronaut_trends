★ Orbital vs suborbital spaceflight over time

Two eras in one chart. For six decades almost everyone who "went to space" reached orbit; since 2021 a wave of suborbital passengers (Blue Origin, Virgin Galactic) has redefined the astronaut count. An astronaut is counted Suborbital only if they never reached orbit. This panel always shows both classes; the Flight filter above drives every other chart.

1 · New astronauts entering service, by year

Each person is counted in the year of their first flight. Bars are stacked by citizenship group. Drag the slider under the chart to zoom into an era; the 2021→ spike is suborbital commercial spaceflight.

2 · Mission-duration mix over time, by country

The original study figure, rebuilt interactively: number of astronauts per first-flight year, split by how long they have cumulatively spent in space. One panel per country; colour runs short→long.

3 · Career time in space — by country

Box & whisker of total career duration (days, log scale) per astronaut. Russia's median dwarfs everyone — a long-duration station legacy.

4 · Career time in space — by decade

The same measure, grouped by the decade of first flight. The spread widens as both ultra-short tourist hops and year-long station stays appear.

5 · Total astronauts by country

Headcount of everyone who has flown, all years combined.

6 · Duration-bin composition

Share of each country's astronauts falling in each duration band (100% stacked).

7 · Country → duration flow (Sankey)

Width of each ribbon is the number of astronauts flowing from a country into a duration band.

8 · Decade ↔ country (Chord)

Chords link decades to the countries that debuted astronauts in them. Arc length is total debuts.

9 · Country–decade network

A force-directed bipartite graph: countries (squares) linked to the decades (circles) in which they sent people up. Edge thickness = number of debuts; drag nodes to explore.

10 · Duration profile (Radar)

Each country's normalised fingerprint across the five duration bands — the shape shows a national spaceflight "style".

11 · Decade × duration heatmap

Density of astronaut debuts across decade and duration band.

12 · Women in spaceflight

New male vs. female astronauts per year. The female share climbs but has never reached parity.

13 · Summary report table

The full aggregated dataset (first-flight year × country × duration band × count). Search, then export from the repo's data/processed/ folder.
